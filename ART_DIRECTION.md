# Original concept art

All three raster images were generated specifically for this deck using the built-in OpenAI image generation tool. No stock or scraped photographs are used. The native Vue/SVG diagrams were authored in code for accurate labels and numerical behavior.

These are concept renderings, not images of existing SpaceX hardware. Infrared emissions in diagrams are symbolic; radiators around 330 K do not glow visibly red. The artwork is cinematic and is not used as a measured engineering drawing.

## public/images/orbital-concept.png

Prompt: Cinematic widescreen premium aerospace investor pitch background, plausible conceptual orbital compute satellite above curved Earth at night; enormous black/silver finned radiator wings distinct from dark blue photovoltaic arrays, compact silver computing bus, laser communication aperture. Satellite right, left dark negative space for white typography. Crisp sunlight, cyan Earth limb, graphite and silver with warm amber accents. No text or logos, no visibly red glowing radiators. Photorealistic 3D concept art, not existing SpaceX hardware.

## public/images/thermal-concept.png

Prompt: Cinematic widescreen close-up of a plausible orbital AI compute satellite; clean engineering cutaway of server cold plates and insulated coolant plumbing leading toward enormous flat white radiator wings, graphite server enclosure, gold multilayer foil, blue Earth curve, deep black negative space at right. Hard sunlight, subtle cyan details and amber indicators. No text, logos, or glowing red radiators. Premium photoreal concept rendering.

## public/images/deployment-concept.png

Prompt: Ultra cinematic photoreal widescreen SpaceX inspired reusable stainless steel spacecraft in low Earth orbit, black thermal tile belly and flap fins, open payload doors with folded compute satellites dispersing along an orbital arc. Earth curvature below, black space above, spacecraft on far right with dark negative space left. Icy blue and platinum light, industrial hard-science-fiction design. No flame plume, logos, text, or watermark. Clearly conceptual.

The prompts above preserve the final creative specifications; generation used the built-in image tool, not an external CLI.
